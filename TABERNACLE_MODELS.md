# Wilderness Tabernacle - Biblically Accurate 3D Models

## Overview
All Wilderness Tabernacle models have been created with **precise biblical dimensions** from Exodus, using an 18-inch cubit (0.5 units in 3D space for visualization).

---

- **Dimensions**: 5 cubits × 5 cubits × 3 cubits (2.5m × 2.5m × 1.5m)
- **Material**: Bronze/brass overlay
- **Four horns** on corners (Exodus 27:2): "And thou shalt make the horns of it upon the four corners thereof"
- **Brazen grating/network** (Exodus 27:4): Semi-transparent mesh visible in model
- **Square shape** as specified in Scripture

### Interactive Features
- Click to view full Scripture references
- Bronze material with realistic metallic appearance
- Horns positioned at exact corners
- Shadows cast for realistic depth

## 6. Ark of the Covenant (Exodus 25:10-22)

### Biblical Specifications
> "And they shall make an ark of shittim wood: two cubits and a half shall be the length thereof, and a cubit and a half the breadth thereof, and a cubit and a half the height thereof. And thou shalt overlay it with pure gold, within and without shalt thou overlay it, and shalt make upon it a crown of gold round about." - Exodus 25:10-11

### 3D Model Features

#### Main Ark Box
- **Dimensions**: 2.5 cubits × 1.5 cubits × 1.5 cubits (1.25m × 0.75m × 0.75m)
- **Material**: Acacia wood overlaid with pure gold inside and out
- **Crown of gold**: Border around the top edge
- **Position**: Most Holy Place

#### Mercy Seat (Exodus 25:17)
> "And thou shalt make a mercy seat of pure gold: two cubits and a half shall be the length thereof, and a cubit and a half the breadth thereof."

- Rests on top of ark
- Place where God's presence dwelt

#### Two Cherubim (Exodus 25:18-20)
> "And thou shalt make two cherubims of gold, of beaten work shalt thou make them, in the two ends of the mercy seat. And the cherubims shall stretch forth their wings on high, covering the mercy seat with their wings, and their faces shall look one to another"

- **Position**: One on each end of mercy seat
- **Wings**: Extended upward and inward
- **Facing**: Looking toward each other
- **Material**: Pure beaten gold
- **Purpose**: Guard the mercy seat

1. **Tables of stone** - Ten Commandments (Exodus 25:16)

3. **Aaron's rod that budded** (Numbers 17:10)

### Significance
- Most sacred object in the Tabernacle
- God's throne on Earth (1 Samuel 4:4)
- Only the High Priest could approach it once per year on Day of Atonement

## Technical Details

### Scale System
- **1 Cubit = 18 inches = 45.72 cm**
- **3D Scale**: 1 cubit = 0.5 units (for optimal visualization)
- All biblical dimensions converted proportionally

### Materials

#### Bronze/Brass
- **Diffuse Color**: RGB(0.72, 0.45, 0.2)
- **Specular Color**: RGB(0.8, 0.6, 0.3)
- **Specular Power**: 64
- Used for: Altar, Laver

#### Pure Gold
- **Diffuse Color**: RGB(1, 0.84, 0)
- **Specular Color**: RGB(1, 0.95, 0.5)
- **Specular Power**: 128
- **Emissive Color**: RGB(0.15, 0.13, 0)
- Used for: Lampstand, Table, Incense Altar, Ark

#### Acacia Wood (under gold overlay)
- **Diffuse Color**: RGB(0.55, 0.4, 0.25)
- **Specular Color**: RGB(0.3, 0.2, 0.1)

### Lighting System
- **Hemispheric Light**: Ambient lighting from above
- **Directional Light**: Creates realistic shadows
- **Point Light**: Central illumination
- **Shadow Generator**: Real-time shadows with blur effect

### Interaction
- **Click any component** to view its Scripture references
- **Highlighted components** glow with gold emissive color
- **Camera controls**:
  - Left-drag to rotate
  - Right-drag to pan
  - Scroll to zoom
